PT. Metindo Perkasa was established in 1969. Initially, we specialized as a supplier for electroplating. We supplied electroplating services to industries, starting with processes like hard chrome and tin cobalt. Over time, as Indonesia’s industries have grown, we have shifted our focus to supplying various surface technologies to meet the current demands of the industry. These technologies include nano ceramic pre-treatment before painting for various metals, tin nickel process, nickel-free process, and more.
